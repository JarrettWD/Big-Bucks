# Avatar artwork

The animals and five of the accessories are from **Fluent Emoji** by Microsoft
(https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), "Color" style, used unchanged under the
MIT licence. The licence is in `LICENSE-fluentui-emoji` in this folder.

Only the files Big Bucks uses are copied here, renamed to the keys the app stores:
